Spaghetti with Tomato Sauce

Enjoy a simple, comforting bowl of spaghetti tossed in homemade tomato sauce with garlic and basil. This easy recipe is perfect for a quick lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 200 grams of spaghetti
  • 2 tablespoons of olive oil
  • 3 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 can of crushed tomatoes (400 grams)
  • A handful of fresh basil leaves
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese, optional

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds, until fragrant.
  3. Add the crushed tomatoes, salt, and black pepper. Simmer gently for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Reserve half a cup of pasta water, then drain the spaghetti.
  5. Add the spaghetti to the sauce and toss well. Add a little reserved pasta water if needed to loosen the sauce.
  6. Stir in torn basil leaves and serve with Parmesan cheese if desired.
